Why Placio exists

Good openings are scattered across dozens of career pages and job boards—many gated behind forced logins or already expired by the time you find them. Placio brings active roles across India into one calm, fast interface built around how people actually search: by department, city, skill, and experience.

How it works

01Filter Without Friction

Narrow roles by department, city, skill, or fresher eligibility in one click.

02Inspect Clean Details

View clear role requirements, location, and salary details with zero popups.

03Apply on Company Site

Click straight through to the employer’s application page—no middlemen.
