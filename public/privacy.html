Stored on your device

Your browser keeps a few things so the site works: saved jobs, recent searches and views, your theme, your privacy choice, and a short-lived copy of job data (up to 24 hours) so pages load faster. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can clear this anytime through your browser settings.

Analytics & advertising

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It does not know your name or contact details. You can opt out anytime with Google’s opt-out add-on. We may also display third-party ads (such as Google AdSense), which use cookies to show relevant ads; you can manage this anytime in Google Ad Settings.

Reporting a job

“Report this job” stores only the job ID, your chosen reason and the time. No personal details.

Apply links

“View & Apply” opens the employer’s own website, which has its own privacy practices.

Hosting

Placio is hosted on Firebase and Vercel, which process standard technical data (such as IP address and browser type) to serve pages reliably and securely.

Questions? We’re happy to help.

Want a job removed or an email conversation deleted? Write to placio.jobs@gmail.com. Placio is intended for users aged 13 and above, and follows India’s DPDP Act, 2023. If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above.
