What Placio does

Placio is a free job-discovery platform that gathers publicly listed jobs and internships across India. We are not the employer or recruiter, we don’t take part in hiring decisions, and we can’t guarantee any outcome.

Employer names & trademarks

Company names, logos and job descriptions belong to their owners and are shown only to help you find the original listing. Placio is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. Owners can ask for a listing to be updated or removed at any time.

Candidate safety & external sites

Please verify a role before you apply. A genuine employer will not ask you to pay to apply, interview or receive an offer. “View & Apply” opens the employer’s or a third party’s own website, which has its own terms and privacy practices. If a listing looks suspicious, use “Report this job” or email us.

Acceptable use

Use Placio fairly: no scraping that slows the site, no attempts to reach admin areas, and no false reports.
