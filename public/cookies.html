Cookie Policy

Placio itself sets no cookies: it uses your browser’s localStorage, sessionStorage and IndexedDB. The only cookies on this site come from Google Analytics (see below). This page lists everything, grouped by purpose.

Necessary

Needed for the site to work: remembering your privacy choice, and a cached copy of the job list (so pages load fast and use less data). These cannot be switched off, and contain nothing personal.

Functional

Saved jobs, recently viewed jobs, recent searches and your light/dark theme. These are only created when you use the feature, they stay on your device, and you can clear them at any time.

Analytics and marketing

Placio uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It sets the cookies _ga and _ga_SBMSLDDXC8 (up to 2 years) to tell visitors apart anonymously. No advertising or marketing cookies are used. To opt out, install Google’s opt-out browser add-on.
